All students at NMIMS should note an important update regarding changes in the Grievance
redressal/ revaluation rules. These changes apply to all students irrespective of their admission
year requesting revaluation of their answers or answer books of the final examinations
conducted by the University. This document will supersede all previous rules as regards
grievance redressal/ revaluation.
The re-evaluation process will involve the following steps:
i)
When a student applies for revaluation, he/ she will have to necessarily make an
application to receive a soft copy/ photo copy of the answer book/ s. Student will
have to go through copy of evaluated answer books before making application for
revaluation. Student can not directly make application for revaluation, without
appropriate reasons for applying for revaluation.
ii)
Students are requested to note that - application for revaluation will get enabled
only after students concerned makes application for receiving copy of answer
book/s.
iii)
While making application for revaluation, the student concerned must specify the
question(s) for which revaluation is requested, along with detailed reasons
justifying the request. Clear and specific reasons must be given as to why he/ she
is seeking revaluation. Revaluation requests without sufficient justification or
clarity will not be accepted, and the corresponding answer script(s) will not be sent
for revaluation.
After all grievance/ revaluation applications are received, University will arrange for the
answer book/s to be revaluated by another subject expert or faculty member. Such expert/
faculty will review the answer book/s specifically for those questions, considering the synoptic
answers and the reasons given/ objections raised by students.
