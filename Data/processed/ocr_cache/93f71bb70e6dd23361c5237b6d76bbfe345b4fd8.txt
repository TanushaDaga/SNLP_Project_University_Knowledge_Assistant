1.4 Dress Code and Grooming:
All students need to come well-groomed for all Placement related activities. Each and every student needs to wear the NMIMS
Formals.
1.4.1 All the students participating in the placement process must have a minimum 2 sets of Formal Attire to avoid any issue of
non-availability of the same in the event of back-to-back drives.
1.4.2 The Placement Cell reserves the right to send a student back to change into proper formal attire if he/she is found not to be
following any one of the above-mentioned parameters or not complying with presentable/acceptable norms of the institution.
For Girls:
Wear plain black trousers, which are loose fitted and teamed with a collared light toned plain white shirt along with
NMIMS Blazer
Checks and Bold stripes on shirts are not allowed
Hair should be neatly combed, pinned up and tied
Closed black formal shoes (no heels / Sneaker/Sports Shoes not allowed)
For Boys:
Wear plain black trousers, which are loose fitted and teamed with a collared light toned plain white shirt along with
NMIMS Blazer
Checks and Bold stripes on shirts are not allowed
Well-groomed hair and beard
Well-polished black formal shoes (no heels / Sneaker/Sports Shoes not allowed)
