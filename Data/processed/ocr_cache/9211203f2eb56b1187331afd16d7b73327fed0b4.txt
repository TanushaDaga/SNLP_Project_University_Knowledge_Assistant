2.2. Application for Job Opportunity
2.2.1. The student shall have to compulsorily sit for all placement drives where he/she has nominated.
Failing to do so, the Placement Cell will consider that student has withdrawn himself/herself from the
complete Placement season and no further placement assistance will be provided.
2.2.2. Students need to nominate through the LineupX Portal only. Strict timeline/deadline would be
mentioned. No time barred application/nomination would be accepted and or entertained. Later, no e-mail
requests would be accepted. Students are advised to follow the time line strictly.
2.3. Coordination with Student Placement Committee Members:
For the recruitment drives, the students are required to co-operate with their respective Student Placement Coordinators as they will
be the intermediaries between the Placement Office and the student body. Students should provide their respective Student
Placement Coordinator with all necessary details as and when requested and specified by the company.
In case the student wishes to communicate anything with respect to the recruitment drives or personal information, they should write
an email to the Placement drive POC.
2.4. Phases
2.4.1 In case of auto-nomination opportunities, if an organization has any specific hiring requirements, the Placement Department
reserves the right to request individual resumes or any additional information from students to facilitate the recruitment process.
2.4.2 The Placement Department will forward the profiles/resumes of eligible students who meet the organization's criteria and
hiring norms to the concerned organization. The same will be communicated to the students via email.
2.4.3 It is mandatory for students who meet the eligibility criteria specified by the company and have received communication from
the Placement Cell to participate in the recruitment process.
2.4.4 Students must ensure they possess clear and updated copies of their Aadhaar Card and PAN Card, with matching names on
both documents.
