Dear Students,
You have now entered the final year of your academic program. This is the time when the Placement Cell lines up the companies
to visit our campus to recruit students for final placements. The Placement Cell works as a facilitator between the institution and the
corporate world. We look forward to have your total dedication and best-efforts during placement drives in order to make the
placement season for the batch of 2027, a success.
The Student Place-coms have been selected who will work in close contact with the Placement Cell on everyday basis, to ensure
that the best opportunities come to the students of our college. However, we consider every student to be an integral part of the
Placement Cell and we look forward for your support in facilitating the best employment opportunities and relevant job profiles to
you and your fellow mates.
Given below, are a few guidelines, which aim to bring clarity to the Placement Processes and Procedure. These guidelines are to
be followed by all concerned which will help in smoothly carrying out the Placement activities for Batch of 2027 at Mukesh Patel
School of Technology Management and Engineering, NMIMS University.
For conductance of smooth placement process/es, the NMIMS University has facilitated the placements through “LineupX Portal",
both web-based and mobile app based, in which the student has to compulsorily register, enroll and get themselves verified, in order
to take the benefit of the opportunities provided by the placement cell.
During the academic year, various Placement activities (viz. Competitions, Industry Insights Series, trainings, etc.) will be organized
and conducted by the Placement Cell with a view to prepare students to be industry ready. Every registered student is required to
participate studiously and obediently in these events.

1. Student Policy
1.1. Placement Communication
All official placement communications will be shared through the LineupX Portal and the student's official NMIMS email ID
1.2. Student Information Gathering
1.2.1 At the beginning of the season, the department would circulate the online Registration Link to collect the details required for
the student profile. Students failing to submit the details would not be considered for verification. Schedule of this activity will be
shared in advance with all the students.
1 . 2 . 2 You are expected to keep all the relevant supporting documents available with you & produce as and when required.
1.2.3 The Placement Department would verify the Academic details with the University and if any inconsistency or mismatch with
respect to the academic details found, the student would be notified. Therefore, the student is expected to take due care while
mentioning marks, CGPA, certifications, etc. as the case may be.
1.2.4 In case there are updates regarding the academic information of a student (e.g.: result declaration, clearing of KT, etc.), it is the
student's responsibility to update the same immediately. Students have to share their latest mark sheet for verification and updating
of records; thus, ensuring the student does not miss any placement drive opportunity.
