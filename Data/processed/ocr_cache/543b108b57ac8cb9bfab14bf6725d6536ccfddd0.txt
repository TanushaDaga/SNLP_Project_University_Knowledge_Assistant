9. Selection Process of Technical Internship Program (TIP)
Students who wish to secure Technical Internship Program (TIP) assistance from college
a)
Registration: Students will be eligible for placements from college Placement Cell, only after
completing LineupX Verification enrolment and acknowledge and accepting the Internship Policy
and norms.
b)
Process Compliance: Students must follow the placement process as per the schedule provided by the
department.
Mandatory Application Requirement: Students opting for internships facilitated by the college must
result in the student's name being Put Out from all future placement opportunities.
d) Non-Rejection Policy: Once a student is selected by a company facilitated by the college, they are
obligated to accept the offer and join the company. Rejection of an offer is not permissible.
10. Procedure for students not Seeking TIP through Placement Department
If one wants to opt-out, he/she should do so before the entire process begins. However, if one has already applied
to companies through campus and are in mid of any process/es through shortlisting/selection, then the student will
have to participate in the further pending process/es and have to opt out of the process/es of further companies.
And in that case if the student gets selected in such a process, he/she will have to take up the offer from campus.
Students who wish to secure Technical Internship Program (TiP) on their own must follow below steps:
Step 1. Apply for Internship OPT Out Form and submit to Placement Cell.
Step 2. Submit Company Details:
a)
Company Profile
b)
Domain & Details of Work / Job Description
c)
HR Contact Information
Step 3. Approval Process:
a)
The submitted details will be verified by the Placement Department and Academic Department.
b)
Upon approval, students must submit the confirmation letter from the industry before deadline given by
the department.
a)
Once the industry confirms the Technical Internship Program (TIP), no changes will be permitted.
