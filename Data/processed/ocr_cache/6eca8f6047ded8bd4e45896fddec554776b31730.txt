B.Tech Integrated Program
Rationale:
In plant Training is an integral part of the B. Tech Integrated Program 4th Year curriculum and provides
exposure to the students about the industry environment and enables the student to put the lessons
learnt in the classroom to practice. As an in plant trainee in Semester Vlll, the students are expected to
obtain practical experience through projects and implementation of technologies as per work allocation
by the industry.
Refer to the IPT dates for Semester Vlll mentioned in the Academic Calendar.
Inplant Training Supervision:
Every Inplant Trainee shall be allocated two Mentors: An Industry Mentor and Faculty Mentor from the
Academic Department. Industry Mentor shall allocate assignment/s, tasks & projects which will be closely
monitored on a daily/Fortnightly basis. Faculty Mentor maintains close liaison with the Industry Mentor
and provides constant guidance based on Fortnightly Reports.
Scope of Inplant Training:
Joining Formality: Mandatory to join on the stipulated Date, and submit the Joining Report to the
Inplant Training Faculty Coordinator within One week of joining.
During the Inplant Training: Abide and adhere to all the rules and regulations of the Industry
related to joining date, work location, timings, leave policy and the duration of the Inplant
Training. Take care of the material items (joining kit) provided to you & use it wisely through the
academic year.
Submit the Fortnightly Progress Report & Final Report as specified by academic coordinator.
Evaluation: Guideline will be shared by the Department Inplant Training Faculty Coordinator.
Student has to inform the placement department if planning to go for IPT by self by last Saturday of July
2022 on the link shared on email.
Obtaining Inplant training in Industry by Self:
Students seeking IPT by self can apply for NOC (via online link) from placements department.
Submit the Details (Company Profile / Domain and details of work/ Contact Details of the HR)
which shall be vetted by the Academic Department.
On approval by the Academic Department submit the confirmation letter from the Industry.
Once the Industry has confirmed the Inplant Training, No changes will be permitted.
Participating in opportunities at College:
Mandatory to participate in all the opportunities for IPT extended by placements department. If
does not participate, the placement opportunities for IPT from college will be withdrawn then the
student will have to obtain IPT on own/self.
On selection the candidate has to accept the Inplant Training offer for the required tenure
assigned by the college & the company in Semester Vlll. No change will be permitted.
Stipend during the Inplant Training (if any) will be provided as per the Industry policy. No
negotiations allowed.
