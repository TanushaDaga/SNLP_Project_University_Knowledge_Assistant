2.9Job Offer
2.9.1 Post recruitment, the company may share the offer letters to selected students directly or via the Placement Cell. In case you receive
the letters directly, you have to submit a copy of the same to the Placement department. Failing to do so, the Placement Cell will take
disciplinary action.
2.9.2 Students are required to note that the Placement Service is an assistance by the Institution and cannot be construed as a
guarantee of a job offer.
2.9.3 It is the responsibility of the student to perform during the Internship tenure if placed for I +PPO so as to retain the job offer
made by the company during the recruitment process.
2.10 A student will be denied further placement assistance in case of one or more of the following reasons:
2.10.1 Academic restrictions
2.10.2 Unprofessional behaviour during a recruitment process
2.10.3 Lobbying with company delegates by self or through others
2.10.4 Establishing parallel channel with company
2.10.5 Negotiating salary / terms (when it is already pre-defined)
2.10.6 Derogatory remarks on colleagues / Institute
2.10.7 Unilateral & Arbitrary withdrawal, midway in the recruitment process
2.10.8 Deliberate underperformance at the designated selection processes during the campus selection
2.10.9 If any student rejecting a PPO (Internship +PPO) will not get further assistance for final placements as it is the responsibility
of the student to perform during the Internship tenure and retain the PPO.
2.10.10 If a student has been placed through an internship with a PPO or a direct placement offer and chooses to exit the
company midway due to unforeseen circumstances, it will be the student's responsibility to secure alternative placement
independently
Certificate from the company.
Note: Student should route any communication to the company via placement department only. In case the student fails to
do so, necessary action would be taken against the student by the Academics.
The Placement Cell reserves the right to change, modify the policy in the best interest of the batch 2027.
