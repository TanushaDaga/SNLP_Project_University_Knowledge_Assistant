B. Tech Integrated Program
Rationale:
In plant Training is an integral part of the B. Tech Integrated Program 6th Year curriculum and provides
exposure to the students about the industry environment and enables the student to put the lessons learnt in
the classroom to practice. As an in plant trainee in Semester XI, the students are expected to obtain practical
experience through projects and implementation of technologies as per work allocation by the industry.
Delivering quality work and timely completion of allocated tasks can lead to PPI/PPO from the
organization.

Inplant Training Supervision:
Every Inplant Trainee shall be allocated two Mentors: An Industry Mentor and Faculty Mentor from the
Department. Industry Mentor shall allocate assignment/s which will be closely monitored on a
daily/Fortnightly basis. Faculty Mentor maintains close liaison with the Industry Mentor and provides
constant guidance based on Fortnightly Reports.
Scope of Inplant Training:
Joining Formality: Mandatory to join on the stipulated Date, and submit the Joining Report to
the Inplant Training Faculty Coordinator within One week of joining.
During the Inplant Training: Abide and adhere to all the rules and regulations of the Industry
related to joining date, work location, timings, leave policy and the duration of the Inplant Training.
Submit the Fortnightly Progress Report & Final Report as specified.
Evaluation: Guideline will be shared by the Department Inplant Training Faculty Coordinator.
Obtaining Inplant training in Industry by Self:
Students seeking IPT by self can apply for NOC (via online link) from placements department.
Submit the Details (Company Profile / Domain and details of work/ Contact Details of the HR)
which shall be vetted by the Academic Department.
On approval by the Academic Department submit the confirmation letter from the Industry.
Once the Industry has confirmed the Inplant Training, No changes will be permitted.
Participating in opportunities at College:
Mandatory to participate in all the opportunities for IPT extended by placements department.
On selection the candidate has to accept the Inplant Training offer for the required tenure assigned
by the college & the company in Semester XI, No change will be permitted.
Stipend during the Inplant Training (if any) will be provided as per the Industry policy. No
negotiations allowed.
Conduct of Inplant Training:
Mandatory to join on the stipulated Date.
Submit the Joining Report to the Inplant Training Faculty Coordinator within One week of joining.
