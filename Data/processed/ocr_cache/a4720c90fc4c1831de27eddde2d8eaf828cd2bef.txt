1. Introduction & Objective
The Technical Internship Program (TIP) is a vital part of the B.Tech/BTI/M.Tech/MCA curriculum, offering
students real-world industry exposure and bridging the gap between academics and practical application. During
the internship, students work on industry-assigned projects, gaining hands-on experience and improving their
professional skills, which can increase their chances of securing a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO). The Placement
competencies, motivation, interview performance and industry fit.

3. Definitions:
a)
Placement Cell: The Placement Cell, which comprises both the Placement Department and Student
Placement Committee, managing all placement-related activities.
b)
Eligibility: The eligibility criteria specified in the job description provided by the recruiting company.
Placement Process: Encompasses all stages of candidate selection, including pre-placement talk,
online/offline assignments, group discussion, and personal interview etc.
d)
Student Placement Committee: The Student Placement Coordinators who act as the point of contact
between the students and the Placement Department, facilitating seamless communication and
coordination.
4. Updating Academic Information
a)
The Placement Cell will collect students' academic, personal, professional and other required details via
LineupX Portal which should be accurate and will be verified.
b)
Once verified, student details will be finalized and shared with recruiting companies.
c)
If there is any update/change to be done in your record. You need to connect with the placement
department.
5.Placement Communication
All official placement communications will be shared through the LineupX Portal and the student's official
NMIMS email ID (@nmims.in). Students are required to regularly check both platforms.
6. Resume Building
a)
Every student must prepare their resume in the format provided by the Placement Department.
b)
Students are required to submit their resume in PDF formats whenever required.
c)
Students are encouraged to continuously enhance their profiles by upgrading their technical skills,
working on projects, certifications, etc.
