Course Objective
This course offers the fundamental skills of journalism utilizing different types of mass media such as
print, audio, audio-visual and web media. It also offers the knowledge about the basics of journalism
such as writing, reporting, news anchoring, simple production processes for the newspaper layout and
photo editing as well as the news-based programs in electronic media and features of digital media.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, students will be able to-
 1. Appraise the role of communication in mass media and basic principies of journalism
2.
 Apply the principles of journalism using print media
3.
Compile the news stories and news-based programs for radio and television nedia
4.
Develop the skill for content development for digital media
