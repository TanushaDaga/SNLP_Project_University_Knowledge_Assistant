2.7Optingout of Placements Procedure
2.7.1. In case a student wishes to opt out from the Final Placement Process, he/she must write an email to the resp. placement
mentor of the Placement Cell and must officially sign out of the Placement Process (by filling the Opt Out form). The reason is to
sort out any glitches that may crop up later.
2.7.2. If one wants to opt-out, he/she should do so before the entire process begins. However, if one has already applied to companies
through campus and are in mid of any process/es through shortlisting/selection, then the student will have to participate in the further
pending process/es and have to opt out of the process/es of further companies. And in that case if the student gets selected in such a
process, he/she will have to take up the offer from campus.
