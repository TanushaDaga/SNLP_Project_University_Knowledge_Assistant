Pre-requisite: Fundamentals of Quantum Physics, Linear Algebra
Course Objective
This course aims at providing adequate exposure and training to the theories and applications of
Quantum technologies. This course will help the students acquire the required skills for
implementing and understanding future quantum technologies, like quantum computers, quantum
sensors and quantum algorithms. This course will aid in independent thinking, giving them scope
of contribution in applying quantum theories in engineering applications.
Course Outcomes
After completion of the course, the student will be able to-
1. Recall the fundamentals of Quantum mechanics and Linear Aigebra
2. Identify and explain various applications of quantum mechanics in engineering domains
3. Experiment with various tools like IBM-Quantum and Python to build Quantum algorithms
and evaluate industry-relevant experimental quantities through theoretical knowledge
4.Design example solutions for quantum applications relevant to the industry
