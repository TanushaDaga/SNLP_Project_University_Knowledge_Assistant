7.Instructionsfor theInterviewProcess
7.1. Campus Internship Process Guidelines
a)
Students must not negotiate stipend, job location, work domain, service agreements, or any other terms
during the interview process, as these details are predefined and shared by the company.
b)
Students should avoid contacting company representatives in any form--in person, verbally, or in writing
at any stage of the interview process in their personal capacity.
Any irresponsible behaviour, including derogatory remarks about other candidates or the institute, will
be taken seriously.
Attendance is mandatory for every recruitment process. Students must report on time as per the schedule
communicated. Late arrivals beyond the last reporting time will lead to disqualification from the
internship season.
7.2. Virtual Internship Process Guidelines
a)
The screen background should be white or neutral, ensuring a distraction-free environment with no
disturbances.
b)
Students must ensure reliable network connectivity and have a fully functional laptop with all necessary
technical requirements (camera, microphone, software, etc.).
c)
In case of emergencies, students should have an alternative device.
d)
The Placement Cell and the company conducting the process will not be responsible for any delays or
disruptions due to technical negligence on the student's part.
Missed appointments will not be rescheduled.
7.3. Absenteeism
a)
Only two-time Absence will be permitted by the Placement Department on Medical Grounds or
Personal issues, with prior consent of the Placement Department.
b)
Absence of the student from any Placement activity due to health issues will have to be substantiated
with appropriate and necessary medical documents. Based on the requirements, department would
verify with doctor / parent / guardian. However, considering the criticality of the situation, the
Placement Cell hopes that the student would realize the importance of the opportunities they are letting
go off. Any absence in future Placement activities due to any reason (health or otherwise), hence would
be termed as the student not being interested in the Placement process, and the Placement Department
would consider that the student has withdrawn from the placement season.
8. Dress Code Specifications
All students need to come well-groomed for all Placement related activities. Each and every student needs to wear
the NMIMS Formals.
8.1 All the students participating in the placement process must have a minimum 2 sets of Formal Attire to avoid
any issue of non-availability of the same in the event of back-to-back drives.
8.2 The Placement Cell reserves the right to send a student back to change into proper formal attire if he/she is
presentable/acceptable norms of the institution.
For Girls:
Wear plain black trousers, which are loose fitted and teamed with a collared light toned plain white
shirt along with NMIMS Blazer
Checks and Bold stripes on shirts are not allowed
Hair should be neatly combed, pinned up and tied
Closed black formal shoes (no heels / Sneaker/Sports Shoes not allowed)
